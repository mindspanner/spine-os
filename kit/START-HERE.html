Spine OS · Install

Let's get you set up.

Everything runs offline and stays on your machine — no account, no cloud. This page has no network calls. Pick how much detail you want; the steps stay the same.

macOS — guided install

  1. 1
    Open the Spine-OS disk image you downloaded, then drag Spine-OS to Applications.
  2. 2
    Open Spine-OS
    The guided wizard opens.
  3. 3
    Follow the wizard — accept the licence, pick a folder, name it, add your brand, choose governance, and connect your AI.
    Optional fields have safe defaults and can be changed later.
  4. 4
    Review the plan, install, and run the self-test. Your dashboard opens when every check is green.
What just happenedYou downloaded a folder of plain files — nothing has run yet.
What happens nowThe wizard copies those files into one folder you choose and brands your dashboard. Offline.
What's nextYou open your dashboard, connect your own AI at your pace, and start working.

Under the hood the app is a thin front-end over install.sh, which resolves one canonical folder, refuses system/home roots, guards path-escape, and self-checks that nothing landed outside your chosen folder.

💡 First time only: macOS may say the app “can’t be opened.” That’s normal — here’s the 20-second fix.

Because this is a fresh download (not yet signed by a big vendor), macOS is being cautious — it is not telling you anything is wrong. You’ll see a message like:

“Spine-OS can’t be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software.”  — or —  “…because it is from an unidentified developer.”

Do this (macOS 15 / Sequoia):

  1. Click Done / OK on that message.
  2. Open → System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to “‘Spine-OS’ was blocked…” and click Open Anyway.
  4. If asked, enter your Mac login password and click OK → then Open.
About that password: it’s your Mac’s own login password, typed into the grey macOS box — macOS just confirming it’s really you. Never type it into a website, an email, or into us. Spine OS never sees it and never asks for it.
On macOS 13–14 (Ventura / Sonoma) it’s even quicker

Right-click (or hold Control and click) Spine-OS → choose Open → in the dialog click Open again. You only do this once.

Still stuck? Two more ways in (both work)

Option B — the plain script

Double-click install.command instead. A Terminal window opens and walks you through the same steps. If macOS blocks it too, use the same Privacy & Security → Open Anyway step above.

Option C — one line in Terminal (always works)

Open Terminal (press ⌘+Space, type Terminal, press Return). Paste the line below, press Return:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Spine-OS.app" && open "/Applications/Spine-OS.app"

Plain English: it clears the download quarantine flag only from the Spine-OS app and opens it.

Prefer to skip the app and install straight away?
K="$(ls -dt ~/Downloads/Spine-OS* ~/Desktop/Spine-OS* ~/Spine-OS* 2>/dev/null | head -1)"; bash "$K/install.sh"

Runs the installer directly; it asks where to put your vault and installs a clean default brand (change it later in status-board.json).

Why does the one-liner clear a “quarantine” flag? macOS tags every download with it. Signing the app removes the prompt for everyone — that’s on our roadmap; until then this is the safe, local, one-time equivalent.

What’s in the box
  • Spine-OS.app — the guided macOS setup launcher.
  • dashboard.html — your branded command centre; opens offline, no build step.
  • status-board.json — the data your dashboard shows (edit it any time).
  • intake.html — tailor your folders + governance (offline, ~1 min).
  • vault/ — the Spine OS vault (Inbox · Projects · Governance · Clients) + starter ADRs & SOPs.
  • SETUP-GUIDE.md — connect your own AI (Claude or GPT), at your pace.
  • _config/brand.local — your brand (name · colour · logo), yours to edit.
