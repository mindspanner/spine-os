# SOP — Case files and notes

- **When:** every new client, every contact.
- **Owner:** the practitioner.

> If your service has an official client record system, **that system is the record** — notes go there.
> Use this folder for your own working files only where your policy allows. Not legal advice.

## Open a case
1. Give the client the next code in `30-CLIENTS/_CODE-KEY.md` (C-0001, C-0002 …).
2. Copy `30-CLIENTS/_CASE-TEMPLATE/` and rename the copy to the code (e.g. `C-0007`).
3. Fill in `00-SUMMARY.md`. Record consent.

## After each contact
1. Write a note within 24 hours from `notes/_NOTE-TEMPLATE.md` (Data · Assessment · Plan).
2. Add any next step to `REMINDERS.md` if you use reminders.
3. File letters and forms in the client's `documents/` folder.

## Using AI with case files
- Ask the AI to work from the client code. It must not copy names, dates of birth, addresses
  or Medicare/NDIS numbers into an online AI service.
- Before anything leaves your computer, ask: "check this for identifying details first".
- Your organisation's policy wins if it is stricter.

## Close a case
Mark the summary closed, note the date, and let the retention clock in
`SOP-record-retention-AU.md` start. Do not delete early.
