# Routine — Email help (draft only)

**The rule:** the AI drafts. You read it, change it, and press Send yourself. Always.

## Ways to use it
- "Draft a reply to this email: <paste>" → draft appears in chat and is saved to
  `00-INBOX/YYYY-MM-DD draft email <topic>.md`.
- "Use the appointment-confirmation template for C-0007, Tuesday 3 pm" → fills a template from
  `systems/email-templates/`.
- "Sort these emails" (paste subjects) → reply today · this week · file · ignore.

## Privacy
- Paste only what the reply needs. Remove dates of birth, addresses, health details unless the
  email truly needs them and your service allows it.
- Connecting an email account to an AI is a later, deliberate step: read-only first, written down
  as a decision in `20-GOVERNANCE/decisions/`.
