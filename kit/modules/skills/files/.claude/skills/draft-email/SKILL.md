# Draft an email

1. Draft only. Never send, never connect to an email account in this step.
2. Use a template from `vault/systems/email-templates/` when one fits.
3. Keep it short, warm and plain. Use first names. No health details unless the person asks.
4. Show the draft in chat and save it to `vault/00-INBOX/YYYY-MM-DD draft email <topic>.md`.
5. End with: "Copy it into your email, check it, and press Send yourself."
