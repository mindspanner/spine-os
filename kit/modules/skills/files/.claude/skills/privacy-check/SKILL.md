# Privacy check

1. Scan for: names, dates of birth, addresses, phone numbers, emails, Medicare/NDIS/DVA/IHI
   numbers, case or file numbers, workplace or school names, rare details that could identify someone.
2. List what you found, then offer a cleaned version with codes (`C-0007`) and general terms
   ("a primary school", "late 30s").
3. Never claim text is anonymous. Say "de-identified as far as I can see — please check".
4. Files in `.spineosignore` never go to an online service, cleaned or not, unless the person says so
   for that one item.
