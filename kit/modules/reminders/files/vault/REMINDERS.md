# Reminders

One list for everything with a date. Your AI reads it when you ask "what's due?".

| Due (YYYY-MM-DD) | What | For (code or project) | Repeat | Done |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-12-01 | Example: renew professional insurance | admin | yearly | |

**Add one:** tell your AI "remind me to … on …".
**Get a calendar alert:** ask "make a calendar file for this reminder" — your AI saves a
`.ics` file in `00-INBOX/`; double-click it to add it to Outlook, Apple Calendar or Google Calendar.
Nothing is sent anywhere; you add it yourself.
