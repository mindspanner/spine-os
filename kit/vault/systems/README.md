# Systems — runnable playbooks

A **system** is a procedure made do-able: open it, follow the numbered steps, and the thing gets done —
by you, or by your AI assistant working from your vault. Where an SOP in `20-GOVERNANCE/` says *what* a
procedure is and *why*, a system here is the line-by-line *do-it-now* version, with a checklist and a
worked example you can copy.

| System | Run it when | Roughly |
|---|---|---|
| [Morning Briefing](Morning-Briefing.md) | First thing each working day | 5 min |
| [Weekly Review](Weekly-Review.md) | Once a week, same slot | 30 min |
| [Client Intake](Client-Intake.md) | The moment a new client says yes | 10 min |

## How to use these

- **Solo:** follow the "Run it yourself" steps and tick the checklist.
- **With an assistant:** copy the "Run it with your assistant" prompt, point your BYO-LLM at the vault,
  then **review its output before acting** — see `20-GOVERNANCE/SOP-llm-assistant-use.md`.
- **Make your own:** these three are starters. When you've done any task three times, copy this shape
  (what / when / steps / checklist / example) and add your own system.

> These are admin playbooks for running a small business, not professional, legal or clinical advice.
> Adapt them to how you actually work.
