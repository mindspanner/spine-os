# Spine OS — Privacy & Data Handling · v0.1 DRAFT

> **DRAFT — not legal advice.** Australian-lawyer sign-off required before live use. Renders template block **D5** (+ D2). Aligns with the Australian Privacy Act / APPs in spirit; final wording to be lawyer-reviewed.

## 1. Local-first by design
The operational data and files you create in Spine OS **stay on your own storage**. By default they are **not transmitted to, collected by, or hosted by Mindspan Pty Ltd**. Mindspan cannot see your vault.

## 2. What Mindspan does receive
- **Enquiry details** you choose to send (e.g. emailing info@mindspan.com.au): used **only** to respond to you, and retained no longer than needed for that purpose.
- **Purchase details** (if/when the funnel is live): processed by the payment provider (e.g. Stripe) under **their** terms; Mindspan receives the minimum needed to fulfil and account for the sale. *(Funnel is currently inactive; no payment data is collected.)*

## 3. Optional, opt-in telemetry
Spine OS does **not** phone home by default. Any diagnostic telemetry is **opt-in** and **de-identified**; you can decline and the product works fully.

## 4. Third-party integrations are yours
Any LLM, payment, scheduling, or email integration you connect is governed by **that provider's** privacy terms. You decide what — if anything — to connect, and what data flows to it.

## 5. Your clients' data
If you use Spine OS to handle **your own** clients' information, **you** are the data custodian for that information and responsible for your professional/legal obligations to them. Spine OS gives you local control; it does not assume custody of your clients' data.

## 6. Your rights & contact
For anything Mindspan does hold (enquiry/purchase records), you may request access or correction: **info@mindspan.com.au**. Final published policy will set out complaint and retention specifics.
